<Section gap={64} divider>

Brought in as the sole product designer on a fully remote team, the challenge was to craft *a tamper-proof, easily auditable marketplace*, in a regulated industry, where investors were the first customers.

The goal? Balancing trust, usability, and the immutability of blockchain *without slowing down the fast-moving nature of trade*.

</Section>

<Section gap={224} title="Discovery">

Blockchain was an intriguing topic. I heard the word here and there, especially in the context of Bitcoin. But for this project understanding *blockchain’s terminology, principles, and real-world application*s was critical to shaping a product delivering value.

To align seamlessly with developers, the first step was defining the blockchain solution's *requirements and its database structure*. With a clear understanding of who the users were, we involved them in task analysis and interviews. That allowed us to understand the details of how selling, buying, and tracking cannabinoids to the end customers worked *to get the job done*.

</Section>

The collected details about tasks showed their complexity: the right job for the ORCA framework, a part of *Object-Oriented UX (OOUX)*. By breaking the system into Objects, their Attributes, and Calls-to-Action, it became easier to understand relationships between key entities and highly increase a chance of a consistent, scalable design.

I learned that it was essential for users to be *compliant with the regulations* of the cannabinoids market in the USA and EU. These included ensuring KYC (know your customer), product quality, and safety standards, such as testing for contaminants, proper labeling of cannabinoid content, and certifications.

</Section>

Integrating all the requirements early in the discovery phase was critical in shaping both the *data structure and smart contract logic* before deployment.

Unlike regular software, mistakes in blockchain logic aren’t easily patched. They often require deploying a new contract or carefully migrating data to preserve integrity and audit trails.

</Section>

<Section gap={164} title="Design" divider>

With a high volume of data and fast-paced transactions, the next challenge was to create an interface that felt powerful yet approachable. We applied best practices to build *information-rich dashboards*, pairing *interactive charts and listings* with a layout that made it easy to scan, search, and take quick action, whether buying, selling, or tracking assets.

The interface adapted seamlessly across *desktop, tablet, mobile*, prioritizing key actions and simplifying layouts where needed. Finally, both *light and dark themes* were incorporated to ensure visual comfort across varied environments using color agnostic design tokens.

</Section>
